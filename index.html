View all FAQs
What is the MiSmile Network?
The MiSmile Network is a nationwide group of independent dentists and orthodontists who provide Invisalign treatment through participating dental practices across the UK.
Is the first Invisalign consultation free?
MiSmile advertises free initial Invisalign consultations at participating practices. Your local provider can discuss your smile goals, suitability, treatment options and costs.
How much does Invisalign cost with MiSmile?
Participating MiSmile practices advertise Invisalign from £38 per month. The total price depends on treatment complexity, your individual plan, deposit and selected finance arrangement. Ask your local provider for a personalised quotation.
How long does Invisalign treatment take?
Treatment duration varies according to individual dental needs and the complexity of tooth movements. MiSmile describes treatment periods ranging from approximately four to 24 months. Your dentist or orthodontist will explain your expected timeline.
Do I need to visit a dentist during treatment?
Yes. Invisalign treatment is supervised by a dentist or orthodontist. Your provider monitors your progress through regular checkups and can address questions throughout your treatment journey.
Can teenagers have Invisalign?
Many MiSmile Network practices offer Invisalign treatment for teenagers. Suitability and treatment options should be discussed with an appropriately qualified dental professional.
How do I find my nearest MiSmile practice?
Visit the MiSmile locations directory and use its practice finder to explore participating providers across the UK. You can then request a free consultation at a suitable practice.
