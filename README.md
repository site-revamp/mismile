# The MiSmile Network – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

The MiSmile Network is a UK-wide network of independent dental practices and orthodontists specialising in Invisalign clear aligner treatment. It connects patients with participating dental professionals while providing network members with clinical support and expertise.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting MiSmile as a trusted Invisalign provider network, helping patients understand the treatment process, explore its benefits and find participating dental practices for an initial consultation.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Improved Treatment Information and Service Discovery

**Previous issue:** The range of Invisalign services and treatment information needed clearer organisation to help visitors understand their options.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Improved the presentation of Invisalign treatment options.
- Organised information around treatment benefits and the patient journey.
- Created clearer pathways for adults, teenagers and children.

**Result:** Visitors can understand the network's Invisalign offering and find relevant treatment information more easily.

### 2. Stronger Network Credibility and Trust

**Previous issue:** The network's scale, professional expertise and relationship with independent dental practices needed clearer communication.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Strengthened the presentation of the MiSmile Network and its nationwide presence.
- Improved the visibility of clinical leadership and professional expertise.
- Highlighted dentist-supervised treatment and participating dental practices.
- Improved the presentation of the network's clinical support and digital treatment approach.

**Result:** The homepage communicates the network's professional positioning and helps visitors understand how its participating practices deliver Invisalign treatment.

### 3. Improved Practice Discovery and Consultation Enquiries

**Previous issue:** Visitors needed a clearer route to find local participating dentists and request an initial Invisalign consultation.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Improved the visibility of practice-finding calls to action.
- Strengthened pathways towards the postcode-based practice finder.
- Made consultation enquiries more prominent.
- Improved navigation towards participating practice information.

**Result:** Visitors can move more easily from exploring Invisalign treatment to finding a participating practice and requesting a consultation.

## 3. Design Improvements

The redesigned homepage introduces the following visual and usability refinements:

- **Visual identity:** Established a more consistent presentation reflecting MiSmile's professional orthodontic positioning.
- **Layout:** Improved section organisation and content hierarchy.
- **Typography:** Refined heading and body text presentation for readability.
- **Visual presentation:** Improved the integration of relevant dental imagery and supporting visual elements.
- **Content consistency:** Created a more structured presentation of treatment information, network expertise and patient guidance.
- **Navigation:** Improved access to treatment information and the practice finder.
- **User experience:** Created a clearer journey from discovering Invisalign to understanding treatment and finding a local provider.
- **Responsive design:** Structured the homepage to support desktop and mobile browsing.

## 4. Final Outcome

The redesigned MiSmile Network homepage provides a clearer presentation of its Invisalign services, professional network and nationwide practice discovery process.

The three main improvements focus on treatment discovery, network credibility and consultation enquiries.

The resulting design helps prospective patients understand the network, explore Invisalign treatment and find participating dental professionals more efficiently.
